The vicious cycle

If ads are your only channel, here's the loop you're stuck in.

Discount ads don't just bring lower-quality leads — they bring a specific kind of student who's gone before they ever commit.

The Ghost

Never shows

Books a trial because it's free and frictionless — then never walks through the door.

The Groupon Crowd

Here for the freebie

Comes for the deal, takes the discounted classes, and disappears the day the offer ends.

The 3-Month Quitter

Gone by month three

Signs up on a promo with no real intent and quits within three months — long before they'd refer anyone.

And the spend never stops feeding itself

  1. Rely on trial & discount ads to fill the calendar
  2. Generate lower-quality leads
  3. Lower-quality leads churn faster
  4. High churn creates pressure to backfill with more ads
  5. More ad spend just to maintain the same numbers
  6. Ad performance gets inconsistent month to month
  7. Owner cycles to a new agency (every 4–5 months)
  8. New agency, same promises — back to step 1

Meanwhile, the school that owns local search keeps growing — without spending on ads.
